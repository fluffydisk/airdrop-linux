# Airdrop 1.9.1

This release includes the current experimental native Quick Share implementation in the portable Linux installer and AppImage. Android-to-Linux transfers were verified on a Galaxy S24+ and Galaxy A13 in specific test conditions. Discovery can still fail, and transfers can stall after acceptance, including on the S24+ with Bluetooth audio active.

To try native Quick Share, launch Airdrop with `AIRDROP_EXPERIMENTAL_QUICK_SHARE=1 AIRDROP_QUICKSHARE_BLE_NATIVE=1` in its environment. Quick Share remains opt-in. Native updates are available through the application's existing update screen on supported portable installations. Package-managed installations use their own update channels.

The GPLv3 Quick Share helper's corresponding source and build instructions are available in [the v1.9.1 source tree](https://github.com/fluffydisk/airdrop-linux/tree/v1.9.1/network/quickshare) and [its build script](https://github.com/fluffydisk/airdrop-linux/blob/v1.9.1/scripts/build-quickshare.js). The portable packages include the helper's license and notices.
