# Airdrop 1.8.1

Fix temporary internet sharing links that could remain stale after a tunnel failure, including Cloudflare Error 1033.

- Automatically replace failed cloudflared processes with bounded retries, backoff and jitter. Wait for the old process to exit before launching another.
- Expire the old sharing QR, pairing capabilities and guest access on interruption. Verify a fresh hostname before displaying Connected or a ready QR.
- Monitor public health even after phone verification. Expire phone verification without fresh authenticated backend health and reject callbacks from old tunnel generations.
- Recognize Cloudflare's Error 1033 in its HTTP 530 response without logging page contents or pairing secrets.
- Preserve Nearby transfers, LAN/Tailscale providers, background preferences, authentication and signed updates.

Quick Tunnel links are temporary. Keep Airdrop running and scan a new QR after recovery. Same Wi-Fi is available if Cloudflare cannot be reached. Phone-verification QR codes remain explicitly pending until real authenticated backend health succeeds. Physical Android validation is not implied by browser-emulation tests.
